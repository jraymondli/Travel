Yes — you’re right. With the schedule you shared, **Sept. 11 and even Sept. 12 can serve as additional backup days** for Lake Louise + Moraine Lake if Sept. 7 and 8 don’t work out.

The flexibility looks like this:

| Date         | Current plan                                 | Can it become a lake day? | Tradeoff                                                    |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sept. 7**  | Lake Louise + Moraine Lake                   | **Primary choice**        | Best fit                                                    |
| **Sept. 8**  | Icefields Parkway → Jasper                   | **Yes**                   | Makes that day longer or shifts Jasper plans                |
| **Sept. 11** | Jasper → Banff/Canmore via Icefields Parkway | **Yes, quite workable**   | You’d need to leave Jasper earlier and reduce Parkway stops |
| **Sept. 12** | Relax around Banff/Canmore → Calgary         | **Yes, excellent backup** | Less relaxed final day, but otherwise little impact         |

Of those later alternatives, I actually think **Sept. 12 is the cleanest backup**. You’re already back in the Banff/Canmore area, and the day was intentionally left light. You could do Lake Louise + Moraine Lake that morning/afternoon, then continue to Calgary afterward.

**Sept. 11 also works**, but it’s less attractive because you’ll be coming all the way from Jasper. Combining the Jasper-to-Lake-Louise drive, the two lakes, and continuing to Banff/Canmore would make for a fairly full day.

So I would now treat your shuttle strategy as:

**Sept. 7 → first choice**
**Sept. 8 → second choice if you can get an early slot**
**Sept. 12 → very strong backup**
**Sept. 11 → additional backup**

That substantially reduces the risk. You don’t really need to panic if Sept. 7 sells out at the two-day release — you have **four possible dates** within an itinerary that already passes through the Lake Louise area multiple times.

In fact, because of that flexibility, I would **not pay a large premium for a private shuttle just because Sept. 7 sells out**. I’d first try the subsequent Parks Canada releases for Sept. 8, 11, and 12.
