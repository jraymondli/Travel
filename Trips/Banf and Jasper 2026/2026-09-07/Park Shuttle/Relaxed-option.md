Yes — **10:00–11:00 AM is still very workable**, especially if your priority is avoiding a very early departure from Calgary.

The key detail is that your **7 hours is roughly the maximum sightseeing window**, not quite 7 full hours *at the lakes*. If you check in around 10:00 and get to your first lake around **10:30–11:00**, you can stay until roughly **6:30–7:00 PM** and still catch a return shuttle before the **7:30 PM last return**. Parks Canada confirms regular return service from both Moraine Lake and Lake Louise runs until 7:30 PM. ([Parks Canada][1])

A comfortable schedule could be:

* **~7:30 AM:** leave Calgary
* **~9:30–9:45:** arrive at Lake Louise Park & Ride
* **10:00–10:30:** check in / board
* **~10:30–1:30:** Moraine Lake
* **~1:30–2:00:** Lake Connector
* **~2:00–6:30:** Lake Louise
* **~6:30–7:00:** shuttle back toward Park & Ride

That gives you roughly **3 hours at Moraine Lake + 4–4.5 hours at Lake Louise**, which is plenty if you're primarily sightseeing and doing shorter walks.

One constraint to remember: the **Lake Connector between Moraine Lake and Lake Louise operates only until 6:00 PM**, so you should transfer to your second lake well before then. The connector runs every 30 minutes and is first-come, first-served. ([Parks Canada][1])

And sunset on **September 7 is about 8:35 PM**, so even if you take the 7:00-ish return shuttle, you'll still be leaving the lakes well before sunset. ([Time and Date][2])

Between the two options, I’d now lean:

**10–11 AM** if you're mainly doing viewpoints, walking around the lakes, lunch, and perhaps one moderate hike.

**9–10 AM** if you want a longer hike such as **Lake Agnes/Big Beehive** in addition to spending meaningful time at Moraine Lake.

Given that you're driving from Calgary that morning, **10–11 AM is probably the more relaxed choice and still gives you a very full day.**
