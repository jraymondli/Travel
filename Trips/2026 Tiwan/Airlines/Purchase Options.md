## My recommendation for you

Best value: STARLUX through Alaska

Choose this if Alaska or STARLUX confirms that you can select two adjacent seats in advance. The rewards value is hard to beat.

Safest booking choice: STARLUX directly

If you can't confirm advance seating through Alaska, book directly. I'd favor 13 hours of comfort and seating certainty over the extra Alaska points.

Choose United if you have a specific reason

For example, you want United MileagePlus credit, already have United status benefits, or prefer United's flight departure times.

One more question that could change my recommendation: Do you currently have elite status with United or Alaska, or are you simply accumulating miles with both programs?

If you're not actively pursuing status, I'd put more weight on STARLUX's economy comfort and convenient seat selection.
