### What I would actually do

Go somewhere like REI and try **Salomon X Ultra 5 Mid GTX and HOKA Anacapa 2 Mid GTX back-to-back** wearing the hiking socks you intend to use.

Because you normally wear around **US 8 running shoes**, start with **8 and 8.5**. Don’t assume 8 is right. On the store’s downhill ramp, your toes should **not touch the front at all**. 

For your TMB setup—luggage transferred, relatively light daypack—I’d choose:

**Salomon X Ultra 5 Mid GTX 8.5** → if it fits securely without squeezing your forefoot.  
**HOKA Anacapa 2 Mid GTX 8 or 8.5** → if cushioning feels substantially better.  
**Merrell Moab 3 Mid Waterproof 8/8.5** → if you need more toe-box room or simply find it the most comfortable.

One more thing: for someone doing a multi-day TMB, **fit is much more important than brand**. A theoretically “better” Salomon that gives you a hot spot after five miles is a much worse TMB boot than a perfectly fitting Merrell.
