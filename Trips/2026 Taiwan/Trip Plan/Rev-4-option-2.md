Tainan and Kaohsiung are quite close, so this is not a huge geographic detour.

By **HSR**, Tainan → Zuoying (Kaohsiung) is only about **15 minutes on the train**, and the fare is **NT$115**. [THSR Pass - Taiwan High Speed Rail台灣高鐵](https://pass.thsrc.com.tw/oatsb2c/home.do?utm_source=chatgpt.com) The practical door-to-door time is longer because Tainan HSR station is outside central Tainan, and Zuoying is north of central Kaohsiung.



For your trip, I would **skip Kaohsiung before I would skip Tainan**.

Why: Tainan gives you something more distinctive relative to the rest of your itinerary — old Taiwan, temples, historic neighborhoods, traditional food, and a very different feel from Taipei/Taichung. Kaohsiung is enjoyable, especially the harbor, Pier-2, and the more modern waterfront, but with your already packed schedule it overlaps more with the “big modern city” experience you will get elsewhere.

So I would simplify the end like this:

**Nov 13:** Alishan → Tainan, spend afternoon/evening there  
**Nov 14:** Tainan morning + lunch → HSR north directly toward Taoyuan/TPE

That would make the trip noticeably less rushed.

I would only reverse that and skip Tainan if you especially want **modern waterfront, harbor scenery, Pier-2, light rail, and a bigger-city atmosphere**. Otherwise, **Tainan is the one I’d keep**.
