### Why I prefer this version

Your **Nov 9 night in Beitou** means the original idea of reaching Taichung on Nov 9 is gone. I would **not try to squeeze Sun Moon Lake into Nov 10** after leaving Beitou. Give Taichung the afternoon/evening on the 10th, then do Sun Moon Lake properly on the 11th.

The Taiwan Tourist Shuttle runs directly from **Taichung/HSR Taichung to Sun Moon Lake**, so this is a straightforward day trip without changing hotels. [Taiwan Trip](https://www.taiwantrip.com.tw/Frontend/Route/Select_p?RouteID=R0005\&utm_source=chatgpt.com)

For **Alishan**, I still strongly favor spending the night there. You can travel from Taichung to Chiayi by HSR and then take a bus or private driver from **Chiayi HSR directly to Alishan**. The official Alishan information confirms direct buses from both Chiayi HSR and Chiayi TRA stations, and reserved bus seats can currently be booked up to **31 days ahead**. [Forest Recreation Taiwan](https://recreation.forest.gov.tw/en/Forest/RA?typ_id=0500001\&utm_source=chatgpt.com)

One useful update from current official information: I would **not plan around riding the entire Alishan Forest Railway from Chiayi to Alishan**. Parts of the main line remain unavailable, so bus/private driver is the more dependable way to get all the way into the recreation area. The mountain branch trains, including the **Zhushan sunrise train**, still operate, with the sunrise departure time announced the previous day. [Forest Recreation Taiwan](https://recreation.forest.gov.tw/en/Forestry/FR?typ=11\&typ_id=0900041\&utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### Nov 14 is the one day I would watch carefully

You can see **some Kaohsiung**, but I wouldn't plan a full sightseeing day there. I'd do something like:

**Tainan morning → Kaohsiung around 12:30–1:00 → sightseeing until roughly 4:30 → HSR north → TPE around 7–7:30 p.m.**

That gives you roughly **3½–4 hours in Kaohsiung** and still a reasonable airport cushion for your **11:20 p.m. United flight**.

If Kaohsiung is important to you, another option is to **leave Alishan earlier on Nov 13, spend only the afternoon in Tainan, then sleep in Kaohsiung**. But I think the itinerary above is better: **Tainan deserves the evening, while Kaohsiung works reasonably well as a shorter final-day visit.**

One thing I'd work on next is **Nov 12 Alishan lodging**. That's now the most important unbooked hotel because staying **inside or right at the Alishan recreation area** makes the sunrise and morning much easier.
