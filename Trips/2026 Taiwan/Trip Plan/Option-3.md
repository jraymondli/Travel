**November considerations:**

- **Yangmingshan:** October–November is silvergrass season. Keep the mountain visit flexible if rain, wind, or fog develops; Beitou provides a useful alternative for more of the day. [taiwan.nps.gov.tw](https://www.taiwan.nps.gov.tw/home/en-us/ThemedArticles/33655?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Alishan:** Stay **inside the forest recreation area or near its entrance**, rather than farther down the mountain, if sunrise is a priority. Bring warm layers and rain protection.
- **Transportation:** I’d prioritize a driver for **November 8** and the **Chiayi–Alishan transfers**. Use HSR between cities.
