# Taiwan Trip — November 7–14, 2026

8 days / 7 nights · Two travelers · HSR + Enterprise rental car

This combines our earlier plans for Taoyuan, Yangmingshan, Beitou, and Taipei with your revised driving itinerary through central and southern Taiwan.

## Complete itinerary

| Date        | Activities and transportation                                                                                                             | Overnight                     |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
