No, you don't have to book Taiwan High Speed Rail (THSR, 台灣高鐵) tickets in advance. You can buy tickets at the station on the day of travel.

However, for your November 10–14 Taiwan trip, I'd recommend booking some tickets ahead of time.

### Your options

| Ticket type             | Advance booking needed? | Seat guaranteed? |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------- |
| Reserved seat (對號座)     | No, but recommended     | Yes              |
| Non-reserved seat (自由座) | No                      | No               |
| Business class          | No, but recommended     | Yes              |

Non-reserved seats are particularly convenient because you can take an eligible train without committing to a specific departure. However, you may need to stand if the train is crowded.

### What I'd recommend for your trip

November 10 — Taipei to Taichung HSR

Book reserved seats a few days ahead. You'll have luggage, and you'll want to arrive in time for your Enterprise rental pickup at noon.

November 14 — Taichung HSR to Taoyuan HSR

Since you plan to return the rental car at noon, I'd wait until November 13 or 14 to choose your train. That gives you flexibility in case the drive back takes longer than expected.

You can purchase reserved seats up to 29 days in advance, or as late as 5 minutes before departure through the official T Express app, subject to availability. Non-reserved tickets are sold only for same-day travel.&#x20;
