## What this means for your itinerary

### November 12 — Tainan

Tainan is where I'd be most careful. Its historic center has narrow streets, numerous scooters, and busy tourist areas.

I recommend booking a hotel with dedicated parking, leaving the car there, and exploring central Tainan on foot or by taxi.

### November 13 — Kaohsiung

Parking should be manageable for your half-day visit. I'd choose a paid parking garage near Pier-2 Art Center or another main attraction rather than spending time looking for street parking.

Budget around NT$100–200 for several hours, although the actual garage rate may differ.
